# speakers never learn

zawinski's law states that "every program attempts to expand until it can read
mail. those programs which cannot so expand are replaced by ones which can". the
replacement part is not really relevant here, as FL Studio is the only DAW that
i can actually use without pulling my hair out or relearning how to walk
currently, but it is relevant.

it has a full video player, it can view HTML documents via Edge WebView2 and
whatever equivalent is on MacOS&mdash;some UI elements are just that, it has 3
versions of a notebook plugin with one being a web browser (just go use a text
editor), and also they're attempting to do Splice but worse. plus there's
automatic mastering&mdash;which most-of-not-all-the-time makes your music sound
bad if you don't want it to fit into a specific genre/sound (reminds me of Dolby
and SoundCloud). you should go learn how to mix and master instead of wasting
your time.

small fries: i swear on lucifer that these developers, lest even the executives
running the show, just sort-of think about the people who actually regularly use
these programs&mdash;the power users&mdash;and mostly let their mind and focus
dwell on the new license-holders who are just getting into this program.
presets, (sample) loops, a prompted LLM, "project jumpstarters"... which most
people won't care about because they already decided to pay for fucking
Omnisphere and Splice, and are avid OTT abusers.

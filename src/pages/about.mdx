# who are y'all, sickos?

<img src="/assets/img/laurelphoto.avif" alt="" class="aboutphoto" />

We mainly publish music under [llia](https://soundcloud.com/x5t), with any unfinished things inevitably landing on our [archive](https://soundcloud.com/8vd).

**Laurel** is a plural system formed in 2022 (fully realised around 2023), with around 10 members or so. We mostly consist of glaceons, cats, and foxes, though our roster is not limited to that list.

It must be noted that this directory only displays members who have a website.

<details>
  <summary>Organisational structure</summary>
  <p>
    We operate without a host, and people take turns fronting weekly (or how long the current fronter lasts).
  </p>
  <p>
    There is memory-sharing, and subsequently people are able to form from existing ones. Most are descendants because of this, and introjects rarely come by.
  </p>
</details>

<details>
  <summary>How to address us</summary>
  <p>
    If you wish to refer to us in our entirety, use "the Laurel system" or simply just "Laurel" (which is less specific).
  </p>
  <p>
    If you wish to refer to a specific one of us, either refer to them by name, or ambiguously as "a headmate of [x]" or by one of our denonyms, <strong>"Laurelite"</strong> and <strong>"Laurelae"</strong> <span class="note">(interchangeable)</span>.
  </p>
</details>

<details>
  <summary>How we address ourselves</summary>
  <p>
    On social networking services, we use "we" to refer to ourselves in whole, and first person pronouns to refer to the one currently posting, the only exception to this being <strong>Twitter</strong>. We do not use individual labels or signatures to signify who is posting; you'll be playing a guessing game on who is <em>actually</em> doing so, and we do not plan to change this.
  </p>
  <p>
    When we (rarely) use messaging apps such as Discord, we don't use PluralKit (or similar), and the above applies unless if explicitly done or priorly stated otherwise.
  </p>
</details>
